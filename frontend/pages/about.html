Our Artisanal Masterpieces

Every Paithani saree in our collection is handwoven by master weavers of Yeola and Paithan, taking weeks to shape pure mulberry silk and genuine zari into royal motifs like Mor-Bangadi (peacock in bangle), Munia (parrots), and Asavali. Our Varanasi Banarasi silks, Kanjeevaram weaves, and handloom cottons represent the finest craftsmanship from across India.
