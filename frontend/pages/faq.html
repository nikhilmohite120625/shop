Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Are your Paithani and Banarasi sarees 100% authentic?

Yes, all our Paithanis and silk sarees are handcrafted by certified artisan weavers in Maharashtra and Varanasi with genuine pure silk and zari certification.

2. How many days does delivery take to my city?

Deliveries within Maharashtra (Tuljapur, Solapur, Pune, Mumbai) take 1-3 business days. Deliveries across South India and other parts of India take 3-6 business days.

3. Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?

Yes, Cash on Delivery is available across all serviceable Indian PIN codes.
